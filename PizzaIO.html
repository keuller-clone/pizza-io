🍕 pizza io

Grow it, bake it, sell it.

Move: WASD or arrows
Interact: E near a station — harvest wheat / tomatoes / herbs / cheese, load or collect the oven, deposit a pizza on the stand, or serve the next customer
The loop: gather all 4 ingredients from their garden beds → load the wood-fired oven and watch the circle fill → carry the finished pizza (on its tray) to the farmstand → serve the customer waiting in line for gold
The cheese plant grows its own wheel of cheese — harvest it when it glows, then wait for it to regrow
Hire help (bottom-right panel): a Harvester tends the garden for you, a Baker runs the oven and ferries pizzas to the stand, a Counter hand serves customers — each can be upgraded for more speed once hired
Upgrade the stand (bottom-left panel): planters, patio tables and lanterns each add a little perk on top of looking cute
Customers are patient, but not infinitely so — don't leave them hanging too long
