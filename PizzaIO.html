🍕 pizza io

Grow it, bake it, sell it.

Move: WASD or arrows
Interact: E near a station — harvest wheat / tomato / herbs / cheese, load or collect the oven, deposit a pizza on the stand, or serve the next customer
The loop: gather all 4 ingredients → load the oven and watch the circle fill → carry the finished pizza (on its tray) to the stand → serve the customer waiting in line for gold
The cheese plant grows its own wheel of cheese — harvest it when it glows, then wait for it to regrow
Hire help (click the panel, bottom right): a Harvester gathers ingredients for you, a Baker loads/unloads the oven and runs pizzas to the stand, and a Counter hand serves customers automatically
Don't dawdle: customers lose patience and walk off if they wait too long
